\caption{Fine-tuning configuration for both backbones. All image operations use
bicubic interpolation and ImageNet channel statistics. Class weights are
$w_c \propto 1/n_c$, normalised to unit mean; this targets balanced accuracy and
deliberately trades simple accuracy for it, which is why all three metrics are
reported. $^{*}$Early-stop patience was initially 12 (ResNet-50) and 10
(ViT-B/16); for the two corpora trained last (Places-LT, Rijksmuseum) it was set
equal to the epoch budget, because a short patience interacts badly with cosine
annealing --- the mid-run plateau is a property of the schedule rather than
evidence of convergence. Food-101, MINC-2500, Multitask-125, NCT-CRC and
OilPainting retain the shorter setting and were not re-run. Every other
hyperparameter is identical across the fourteen corpus $\times$ backbone cells.
A shorter patience can only weaken the fine-tuned baselines, so where it remains
it works in favour of the fusion results we report; and in Places-LT, where the
full budget was available, the selected epoch was still 8 of 75 (ResNet-50) and
6 of 40 (ViT-B/16), indicating that early stopping was not the binding
constraint there.}
